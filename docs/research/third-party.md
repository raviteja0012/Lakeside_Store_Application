# Third-party and connected services: feature surface

Researched 2026-08-12 against the vendor's live documentation, not from memory. 67 capabilities.

**This is a reference catalog, not a plan.** What we adopt and what we decline, with reasons,
lives in [CAPABILITIES.md](../CAPABILITIES.md). The services actually wired up today live in
[PLATFORM.md](../PLATFORM.md). Re-check anything here before acting on it: vendor pricing and
feature names move, and this is a dated snapshot.

Scope note from the researcher:

> Robinson's General Store (`robinsons-store`) — Next.js App Router on Vercel + Supabase Postgres/Auth/Storage + Anthropic API. Survey of third-party services already connected (Vercel, Supabase, Anthropic, Resend, GitHub, Jira, AgentMail, Vianet) and easily connectable ones (Vercel Marketplace catalog, Slack API, Google Workspace APIs). All facts fetched from vendor docs/pricing pages on 2026-08-12; anything not verifiable from a first-party page is flagged in plan_or_cost.

## Google Workspace APIs

| Capability | What it does | Plan or cost | Maturity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gmail API** | Read, send, label and watch mail programmatically: messages.get, messages.send, messages.list, threads.get, drafts.send, history.list for incremental sync, and watch for push notifications via Cloud Pub/Sub. The push model is the alternative to polling IMAP. | 'All standard use of the Gmail API is available at no additional cost', with the same 2026 charging plan flagged for over-quota use. Quotas: 1,200,000 units/min per project, 6,000/min per user. Method costs: messages.get 20, messages.send 100, messages.list 5, threads.get 40, history.list 2, watch 100. | **Google Drive API** | Programmatic file and folder access: upload, download, copy, list, share and permission management, including export of Google-native files to other formats. Metered in quota units rather than raw request counts. | Free today. Quotas: 1,000,000 quota units/min per project, 325,000/min per user, 1 TB/day egress per project. Per-method cost: read 5 units, list 100, download 200, edit 50. Upload ceiling 750 GB/day per user, 5 TB max file size. As of 2026-05-01 projects may use up to 400,000,000 quota units/day before charges apply; Google says full billing details come later in 2026 with at least 90 days' notice. | GA |
| **Google Sheets API** | REST API to create spreadsheets, read and write cell values (spreadsheets.values get/update/append plus batch variants), and apply formatting, conditional formatting, charts, pivot tables, filters, named ranges and protected ranges via spreadsheets.batchUpdate. Supports A1 and R1C1 notation and Connected Sheets. | Free today. Quotas: 300 read requests/min per project and 60/min per user; 300 write requests/min per project and 60/min per user. Google's docs state exceeding quota is PLANNED TO INCUR CHARGES to the Cloud billing account later in 2026 — a scheduled change worth tracking. Recommended payload ceiling 2 MB. | GA |
| **Google Sheets hard limits** | A spreadsheet caps at 10 million cells across all tabs combined and 18,278 columns; a single-column sheet can reach 10 million rows, a 26-column sheet roughly 385,000. Import is capped around 100 MB and new-row insertion at 40,000 at a time. | Free. Not a constraint at this project's scale (thousands of rows a month), and well above what the existing xlsx-based Export screen produces. The 10-million-cell figure is from third-party summaries, not the official quota page — treat as indicative. | GA |
| **Google Workspace plan pricing** | Business Starter (30 GB pooled storage/user), Business Standard (2 TB/user), Business Plus (5 TB/user), Enterprise (5 TB/user, expandable). Starter, Standard and Plus cap at 300 users; Enterprise has no cap. | Reported at $7.00 / $14.00 / $22.00 per user per month on annual commitment and $8.40 / $16.80 / $26.40 on flexible monthly. NOT VERIFIED FROM THE OFFICIAL PAGE — workspace.google.com/pricing did not render dollar figures on fetch, so these come from third-party 2026 summaries. Sheets and Drive APIs work with ordinary free Google accounts; only domain-wide delegation needs a paid Workspace domain. | GA |
| **Google auth: OAuth 2.0 client IDs and API keys** | OAuth client IDs make the app request a specific user's consent to reach that user's own data, with separate client IDs required per platform (web, Android, iOS, desktop). API keys give anonymous access to public data only — files shared as 'Anyone on the Internet with this link'. | Free. OAuth means a consent screen, a refresh-token store and a re-consent path when the token is revoked — three states a non-technical owner would have to be walked through. That cost, not the money, is what rules it out here. | GA |
| **Google auth: domain-wide delegation** | A Super Admin in the Google Admin console authorizes a service account to impersonate any user in the Workspace domain, so it can reach Gmail and Calendar data across users without individual consent. Required for any Gmail access that is not the signed-in user's own. | Free, but requires a paid Google Workspace domain and Super Admin action — neither exists here, since the store's mail is on Vianet. Also a broad grant: it is the key to every mailbox in the domain, which is the same objection DOMAIN_EMAIL.md raised against putting a Vianet mailbox password in Vercel env. | GA |
| **Google auth: service account (no delegation)** | A robot account with its own key that authenticates without any human consent screen; you grant it access by sharing a specific Sheet or Drive folder with the service account's email, exactly as you would a colleague. No administrator privileges required. | Free. This is the only Google auth model that works cleanly for a server-side Next.js route on Vercel with no interactive user, and it covers Sheets and Drive on explicitly shared files. It does NOT reach Gmail. The private key would live in Vercel env, never in the repo. | GA |

## Resend / transactional email

| Capability | What it does | Plan or cost | Maturity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Resend Broadcasts, Audiences and Contacts** | Marketing-side product: contact lists (Audiences), individual Contacts with created/updated/deleted webhook events, and Broadcasts for bulk campaigns to a list. Separate pricing track from transactional. | Marketing plans are priced by contact volume: Free through Pro spanning $0 to $650/mo for 1,000 to 150,000 contacts. No requirement in this project's ledger calls for it — a general store sending vendor invoices is not running campaigns. | GA |
| **Resend Inbound (email receiving)** | Receive mail either on a Resend-managed <anything>@<id>.resend.app domain or on your own domain by pointing an MX record at Resend; you then subscribe to the email.received webhook event. Every address at the domain is received, so routing is done by inspecting the `to` field in the payload. | 'Inbound emails' is listed as included on every plan including Free — no separate inbound price published. This is the second-choice mechanism in DOMAIN_EMAIL.md section 4 (one fewer vendor, since Resend already sends the daily alert). | GA (shipped Nov 2025) |
| **Resend MCP server** | Resend publishes an MCP integration so an agent can send and manage mail through the Model Context Protocol rather than raw HTTP. Listed in the docs navigation alongside the SDKs. | Free with an API key. Details of the tool surface not fetched, so treat the tool list as unverified. | GA (not verified in depth) |
| **Resend Received Emails API and Attachments API** | The email.received webhook carries metadata only — sender, recipients, subject, attachment IDs and filenames — never the body, headers or attachment bytes. You must call the Received Emails API and Attachments API separately to pull content, a design chosen because serverless request bodies are size-limited. | Included with Inbound. Practical consequence: an invoice-intake route on Vercel is a two-hop flow (webhook, then fetch), and mail persists in the Resend dashboard for later retrieval even if the endpoint is down. Retention is 30 days on Free, Pro and Scale. | GA |
| **Resend attachments, SMTP relay and React Email** | Send file attachments, relay through SMTP instead of the REST API, and compose templates as React components via React Email. React Email fits this codebase since it is already React 18 + TypeScript. | All plans including Free list 'RESTful API, SMTP relay, official SDKs, inbound emails, batch sending'. Maximum attachment size not verified — the knowledge-base URL for it 404s. | GA |
| **Resend batch, scheduled send and idempotency** | Batch endpoint sends many messages in one call, scheduled send defers delivery to a future time (surfaced as an email.scheduled event), and idempotency keys prevent duplicate sends on retry. Idempotency is the one to care about here: a Vercel cron that retries must not double-send due-date alerts. | All included on the Free tier. | GA |
| **Resend domains with SPF, DKIM and DMARC** | Add a sending domain and Resend issues the SPF and DKIM records to publish; DMARC guidance is documented. This is step 3 of DOMAIN_EMAIL.md: verify robinsonsgeneralstore.ca so alerts send as alerts@robinsonsgeneralstore.ca rather than a generic address. | Free: 1 domain. Pro ($20 or $35/mo): 10 domains. Scale ($90/mo and up): 1,000 domains. One domain is enough here, so this costs nothing — but the DNS records go in via a Vianet phone call. | GA |
| **Resend send-email API** | REST API at api.resend.com with SDKs for Node, Next.js, Express, PHP, Laravel, Python, Ruby, Rails, Go, Rust, Elixir, Java, .NET and a CLI. Requires a Bearer key and a User-Agent header — requests without User-Agent are rejected with 403. | Already in use for the daily /api/alerts due-date email. Free tier covers it. API rate limit is 10 requests per second per team by default across all plans. | GA |
| **Resend sending regions and data residency** | Sending region is chosen per domain at creation from four options: North Virginia (us-east-1), Ireland (eu-west-1), São Paulo (sa-east-1), Tokyo (ap-northeast-1). There is no Canadian region, and the docs state account data is stored in the United States regardless of the region chosen. | No cost, but this is a stated-fact risk for this project. PLATFORM.md declines Vercel Blob partly on Canadian data residency grounds; Resend already carries the same exposure for anything that passes through email, and inbound invoice bodies and attachments would be stored in the US. Worth writing down explicitly before wiring Resend Inbound. | GA |
| **Resend transactional pricing ladder** | Free: $0, 3,000 emails/mo, 100/day cap, 1 domain, 30-day retention, inbound included. Pro: $20/mo for 50,000 or $35/mo for 100,000, no daily cap, 10 domains. Scale: $90/mo (100k) up to $1,150/mo (2.5M), 1,000 domains, optional dedicated IP at $30/mo once over 3,000 emails/day. | Free tier is adequate today (one daily alert email plus low-volume inbound). The 100/day cap is the only realistic ceiling. Paid plans also include 10,000 automation runs/month. | GA |
| **Resend webhooks** | Email events: sent, delivered, delivery_delayed, bounced, complained, opened, clicked, failed, scheduled, suppressed, received. Plus domain.created/updated/deleted, contact.created/updated/deleted, and suppression.added/removed. Signature verification is Svix-based; automatic retries on endpoint failure. | Included on all plans including Free. | GA |

## Slack API

| Capability | What it does | Plan or cost | Maturity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Slack Block Kit** | JSON UI framework of blocks, block elements and composition objects (header, section, image, fields, buttons, menus) usable in webhook messages, chat.postMessage, modals and the App Home tab. Block Kit Builder is a drag-and-drop sandbox for prototyping the JSON without writing it. | Free. Practical: a daily alert or a per-deploy notification is nowhere near these limits; a per-row notification loop would hit them immediately. | GA |

| **Slack incoming webhooks** | Create a Slack app, enable incoming webhooks, and 'Add New Webhook to Workspace' mints a URL of the form https://hooks.slack.com/services/T…/B…/… bound to exactly one channel. | **Slack plan pricing** | Free ($0, 90-day message history, 10 app integrations, 1:1 video only, 5 GB storage), Pro ($8.75/user/mo monthly or $7.25 annual, unlimited history and apps), Business+ ($18/user/mo monthly or $15 annual, advanced AI, conditional workflow branching), Enterprise+ (custom, enterprise search). | **Vercel app for Slack** | Official Marketplace integration that links Vercel deployments and preview-deployment comments to Slack channels, with threads synced both ways and a Resolve button in Slack. Commands: /vercel subscribe (per-project, filterable by branch regex and page glob), /vercel login to link accounts and get DMs, /vercel whoami, /vercel unsubscribe, /vercel help. | Free integration; costs whatever Slack plan you are on.
