Why each line sits where it does

  • Summer order timeline, Dry Goods only. It came from the bookings sheet, which is the gift and clothing side. Hardware buys garden stock seasonally too, so this is the line I am least sure about. Tell me if Hardware should have it.
  • Location of the order, Dry Goods and Hardware. Gift shows and rep visits are how those two get bought. Grocery is delivery-route driven.
  • Minimum order and reorder, the three merchandise departments. These are the ones where you place an order and a minimum can block it.
  • Payment terms, not on the light form. Your Bakery and Meat tabs do not carry terms; Local Vendors does. If your meat supplier does have terms worth recording, tick it.
  • Specializes in, Property Maintenance. New. Today it is recorded on each invoice, so there is no way to ask who your electrician is.

What happens to what is already entered

Nothing is deleted, ever

  • If a bakery vendor already has a summer order timeline saved from before, it stays saved and it still shows on the vendor page. A question that has an answer is never hidden.
  • The change is only about which questions get offered on a blank form.
  • No database change, no re-entering, and nothing to run.
  • There is an "add the other questions anyway" link on every vendor, for the odd case that breaks the rule.

The same list fixes a second thing

Writing the questions down once, per department, also fixes something you have not reported yet but would have.

Right now if you ask the store "when do I place the summer order for Lawson", it says that is not on file, even when it is. The page that answers questions was never told to look at those fields, and neither was the reorder summary or the vendor directory. One list, used everywhere, means a question that is asked on the form is a question the whole app can answer.

The one question at the end

Today the minimum order must be answered every single time a vendor is saved, even when you only opened the vendor to fix a phone number. You asked for that, back when the field was new, so I am not changing it without asking.

Option A. Keep it. You are asked every time. It does mean the minimum gets recorded rather than skipped forever, which was the original reason.

Option B. Only ask when the ordering questions are on screen. So a Dry Goods vendor still asks, a bakery never does, and correcting a phone number saves without an argument.

My recommendation is B, now that the questions are department specific anyway.
